Personal profile:

I am a 3D computer vision scientist with several years of industry experience and a strong background in camera sensors, optical imaging systems, and 3D photogrammetry. My work spans sensor calibration, multi-sensor integration, and 3D reconstruction, with a focus on building accurate, reliable imaging systems for practical applications.

I also develop AI infrastructure and optimized inference pipelines, including model deployment with TensorRT, custom kernel fusion, and TensorRT plugin development. Using C++, Python, and CUDA, I optimize models and algorithms for low-latency execution on hardware with limited computational resources.

My CUDA development experience covers 3D algorithms for ray tracing, mesh manipulation, mapping 2D segmentation results onto 3D geometry, and noise filtering. I use CUDA–OpenGL interoperability for real-time rendering, connecting GPU computation with interactive 3D visualization. Together, these skills allow me to build complete pipelines from image acquisition and reconstruction to AI inference and visualization.
